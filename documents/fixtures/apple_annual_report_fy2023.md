# Apple Inc. — Sample Annual Report (FY2023 Demo Filing)

## Item 1. Business

Apple Inc. designs and sells iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables and Services. Services growth
continued as the Company expanded subscription offerings.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Key risks included macroeconomic pressure on consumer demand, Greater China competition,
supply chain disruptions, and regulatory risk around App Store practices.

AI was mentioned primarily as an emerging capability area rather than a core disclosed
platform strategy.

## Item 7. MD&A

Net sales were $383.3 billion. Gross margin was 44.1%. Management emphasized installed
base monetization through Services and careful capital returns via buybacks and dividends.

Capital allocation remained shareholder-return heavy with comparatively limited discussion
of large-scale AI infrastructure spend versus later years.
