# Apple Inc. — Sample Annual Report (FY2024 Demo Filing)

## Item 1. Business

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets,
wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The Company’s fiscal
year is the 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

Principal products include iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables, Home and Accessories.
Services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content, and payment services.

The Company believes Artificial Intelligence will be a durable growth driver across its
ecosystem. On-device and private cloud models are intended to deepen engagement in
iPhone and Services while protecting user privacy.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company’s business, reputation, and results of operations are subject to risks including:

- Global and regional economic conditions may adversely affect consumer demand for
  premium devices.
- The Company faces intense competition in hardware, software, and services, including
  from companies with significant AI investments.
- Supply chain concentration and component shortages can disrupt production.
- Regulatory actions regarding App Store practices, privacy, and competition may reduce
  Services growth or increase compliance costs.
- Failure to execute AI product initiatives competitively could impair hardware upgrade cycles.
- Foreign exchange volatility and geopolitical tensions may pressure international revenue.

## Item 7. Management’s Discussion and Analysis

### Net Sales

Net sales were $391.0 billion, up 2% year over year, driven by growth in Services and
iPhone. Americas remained the largest geographic segment. Greater China sales were
mixed amid a competitive smartphone market.

### Gross Margin

Gross margin was 46.2%, reflecting Services mix and favorable cost trends, partially
offset by product mix. Management expects quarterly gross margin to remain in a
similar range, subject to foreign exchange and product mix.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources

Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled approximately $162 billion.
The Company returned significant capital to shareholders via dividends and share
repurchases. Free cash flow remained strong, supporting capital allocation flexibility.

### Artificial Intelligence Strategy

Management highlighted Apple Intelligence as a multi-year platform investment.
Priorities include on-device foundation models, Private Cloud Compute, developer APIs,
and tighter integration across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Management framed AI as a
catalyst for hardware refresh and Services attach rather than a standalone product line.
